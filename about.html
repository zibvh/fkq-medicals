Our Story

Driven by Accuracy

Since 2020, FKQ Medicals has been the go-to diagnostic centre for families and healthcare providers across Ogbomoso. We combine modern equipment with genuine human care.

Who We Are

FKQ Medical Diagnostic Hub Limited is a fully licensed diagnostic laboratory located in the heart of Ogbomoso, Oyo State. We provide a comprehensive range of diagnostic tests and health screening services to individuals, families, healthcare providers, and corporate organisations.

Our laboratory is equipped with state-of-the-art diagnostic instruments operated by trained and certified medical scientists. We are committed to delivering accurate, timely, and affordable results to support clinical decision-making and promote preventive healthcare.

Every result we issue carries the weight of a commitment — a commitment to the patient's health and to the trust placed in us by their doctors.

Our Journey

2020

FKQ Medicals Founded

Opened our doors at Lion junction, Ogbomoso with a small but dedicated team and a clear mission: accurate diagnostics for every patient.

2021

COVID-19 Testing Added

Introduced RT-PCR and Rapid Antigen tests during the pandemic, serving the Ogbomoso community and issuing certified results.

2022

Expanded Test Menu

Grew our services to over 50 tests, adding hormonal assays, histopathology, and corporate health screening packages.

2024

Home Collection Launched

Extended our reach with door-to-door sample collection for patients who cannot visit the lab in person.
